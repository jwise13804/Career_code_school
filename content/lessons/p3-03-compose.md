## What this lesson covers

- `compose.yaml` services, ports and volumes
- Adding PostgreSQL as a second service
- `docker compose up` and `down`
- Configuration through environment variables

```analogy
If one container is a till, Compose is opening the whole shop: tills, stockroom and lights, with one switch.
```
