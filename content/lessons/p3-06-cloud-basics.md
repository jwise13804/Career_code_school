## What this lesson covers

- Renting computers instead of owning them
- IaaS, PaaS and SaaS with everyday examples
- Regions, availability and scaling up or out
- Who's responsible for what: the shared responsibility model

```analogy
The cloud is like a serviced office: you rent exactly the desks you need, the building is maintained for you, and you can add a floor when the team grows.
```
