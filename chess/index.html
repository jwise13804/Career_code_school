Chess Camera

Point your camera at a real board. It follows the game, tells you if a move is legal, rates it, and suggests what to play.

1. Camera

Camera is off

Start the camera, then hold your phone steady above the board (a tripod or a stack of books works best).

Tips and settings
  • Shoot from as high above the board as you can, with even light and no strong shadows.
  • Tap the four outer corners of the playing area in the order shown: a1 (White's queen-side rook), h1, h8, a8.
  • Set up the start position (it should match the digital board), then press Start tracking.
  • Make a move and take your hand away. After about a second the move is read and checked.
  • If a move is misread, press Undo and tap the move on the digital board instead.
